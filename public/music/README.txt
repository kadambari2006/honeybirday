HOW TO ADD YOUR FAVORITE MUSIC TRACKS:
=======================================
1. Place two MP3 music files inside this folder:
   - `birthday-joy.mp3` : Playful, cheerful, upbeat music for celebration
   - `soft-piano.mp3`   : Soft, gentle emotional piano/acoustic music for letter & wishes

2. The website will automatically play them!
   (Even if you haven't placed mp3 files yet, the website's built-in musical chime synthesizer plays enchanting tunes automatically!)

3. You can change file names or paths in `src/data/birthdayContent.js` under the `music:` property.
